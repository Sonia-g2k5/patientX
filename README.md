PatientX is an AI-powered medical data marketplace that allows patients to securely share their anonymized medical records with researchers through consent-based access.

It uses OCR to extract medical information, neural networks to support anonymization and data validation, and AI to simplify medical data processing.
Why is Prediction Used?
Prediction is used to estimate the monetary value of medical records based on factors such as:
- Hospital credibility
- Disease category and rarity
- Treatment details
- Data completeness
- Medical record quality
We use Linear Regression to predict the estimated price based on these features.

Example: A complete medical record containing detailed treatment information may receive a higher estimated value than an incomplete record.

In simple words: PatientX transforms unused medical records into valuable research resources while maintaining patient privacy and consent.
